# Foundations
## Angle Perception
- need to be equal distance or futher away from angle than opponent
### Peeker's Advantage
- you are at disadvantage if peeker uses AD
## Types of positions
- common angles
- off angles
	- unusual positions
	- can turn common into off by standing further away
	- one and done
		- off angle without escape plan
		- used on eco,half buy or bonus 
		- use with get out utility

## How to Hold
- hold as far back as possible
- short jiggle peeks
- holding very tight
	- use one tap weapon or sniper
>[!danger] do not hold 90 degree angle, like in a corner
- rather hold diagonal 
### Gathering Info
- knife jiggle or jump peek before deciding
	- peek off of info
- use when defending multiple choke points alone
	- in common angle where you can get prefired

# Advanced Methods
- go for surprise peeks rather than holding passive angle on bombsite
	- abuse peeker's advantage
	- in choke points
	- when they need to clear multiple angles
## Ready Up Protocol
- remind yourself you are anticipating a gunfight
	- all focus on angle you are holding, crosshair
	- important in first 10s
	- track enemy movement and engagement patterns
- crosshair placement
	- if no info on peeking
		- lots of util = wide swing
		- nothing = close peek
	- jumpspot
		- close to wall, higher than head level
	- jiggle 
		- close to wall, but no too close
	- wide swing
		- wide from wall based on reaction time
	- rule of thumb
		- hold wider rather than tighter
	- holding tight
		- crosshair against opposite wall from enemy peek
	- if stuck between angles
		- gamble on an angle, don't hesitate
- shoot = get behind cover 
	- if in one and done
		- shoot = move and shoot again
## Golden Rules
- dont be exposed to more than 2 angles at once
- rather die trying to reposition to good angle than die on bad angle
## Drop Down Technique
- when playing on elevated spot
- play on edge so you can drop off angle after shooting
- look down while you drop
## Hugging Wall
- crouch hold right against wall
- expose half of body 
- burst then move
## Wallbang
- see tight angle and then spray thru wall
- super thin line
- put crosshair inside of the wall to try get some damage

## Playing alone 
- done when playing with close range weapon
- break util with pistol to bait them 
- switch to shotgun/stinger
